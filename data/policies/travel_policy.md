# Fictional Company Travel Policy

**Purpose:** Synthetic knowledge source for the AI QA Framework portfolio project. This is not a real employer policy.

## Hotel accommodation
Employees may claim up to **INR 3,000 per night** for hotel accommodation. Any expense above the limit requires separate written approval and is not automatically reimbursable.

## Claim deadline
Travel claims must be submitted within **15 calendar days** after travel is completed.

## Receipts
Original receipts are required for reimbursement claims.

## International travel
Employees must obtain manager approval before international business travel.
